From a detail to a keepsake.

A simple creative ritual, with the final choice in your hands.

  1. Choose

    Open a wallpaper or select a video using the system picker.

  2. Make

    Adjust the available controls and preview your composition.

  3. Save

    Save to Photos. To set a wallpaper, use the iPhone system wallpaper controls.

A word about movement

A Live Photo in Photos and movement on the Lock Screen are different things. Lock Screen playback depends on iPhone, iOS, and the exact photo/video pair. Not every work will animate, and FrameLive does not switch wallpapers automatically.

Your creativity stays close.

Editing happens on your device, without an app account, ads, or tracking SDKs. Apple services can handle downloads, payments, photo sync, and backups according to your settings.
