Sources & permissions

Development may involve original artwork and programmed visuals, AI-generated or AI-assisted images and video, and third-party material. Third-party use must follow the specific licence or other lawful basis, including its scope, attribution and distribution conditions. A credit or source label alone does not establish permission.

AI & reuse

Being publicly available online or made with AI does not automatically authorise commercial use or remove copyright, likeness or trademark concerns. Availability in FrameLive does not itself grant unrestricted redistribution or commercial reuse. AI authorship and protection depend on the facts and applicable law; an AI label or a promise to remove content is not permission or an exemption from liability.

Media you import

Import only media you own the necessary rights to use, or have permission or another lawful basis to use for your intended editing, saving and sharing. Your responsibility for imported media does not replace the operator’s own legal duties for the content and services it provides.

Reporting a rights concern

For a copyright, likeness or trademark concern, identify the work by its ID, title or URL and describe the relevant rights, your relationship to them, and the use you believe is problematic. Share only information safe to make public, such as an existing public source link or a short account of your authority. Do not post identity documents, private originals, receipts or orders; these are not required in a public issue.

Review & action

We review the information provided. Where an initial review gives reasonable grounds for concern, we may pause display or distribution, remove the work or correct its presentation while checking the facts. We retain an opportunity to verify the claim and consider clarification or objections from affected parties. Copies already saved to Photos or Files cannot be remotely recalled; you manage those copies on your device.

Your legal rights

This notice does not exclude or limit statutory rights or remedies, liability for unlawful intentional conduct or gross negligence, or any liability that applicable law does not allow us to exclude or limit. A source statement or removal action does not by itself resolve a rights claim.

Use the rights-report link below to open a public GitHub Issue. Submission requires a GitHub account and sign-in. No private email channel is currently provided; keep sensitive evidence private. You may add safe clarifications or objections to the issue. This channel does not replace any legal remedy available to you.
