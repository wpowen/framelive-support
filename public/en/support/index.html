Is creation free?

Yes. Local creation and wallpapers are free. Optional, voluntary one-time support uses Apple In-App Purchase and does not unlock content or features. No subscription is required.

How do I save a wallpaper?

Open a work, preview it, and use its save action. Allow adding to Photos when asked. If you decline, preview and creation remain available; change permission later in iPhone Settings.

Why is my Lock Screen still?

Saving to Photos does not guarantee Lock Screen movement. Use the system wallpaper controls and check whether Live is available for that specific work. Support depends on the exact media, iPhone and iOS. App playback or a Live label alone does not prove wake playback.

Does FrameLive change wallpapers automatically?

No. Choose and set wallpapers using the iPhone system controls. FrameLive does not provide scheduled or automatic wallpaper switching.

Does editing use cloud AI?

No. Your chosen videos are edited locally and are not sent for remote AI generation. Preset artwork or video may have been made with AI during development; other motion is generated by on-device rules.

Do downloads need internet?

Built-in works and local editing can be used without the wallpaper download service. On iOS 26 or later, additional works may be delivered by Apple Background Assets and need a network connection. If a download fails, check your connection and available storage, then use the app’s retry action.

Where are my drafts and finished files?

Open Create → Exported to browse local finished works and open a read-only preview. To delete a local finished work, use its delete action and explicitly confirm. Deletion removes that in-app finished work, not its draft or copies already saved to Photos or Files; manage those copies in their respective apps. Uninstalling also removes local app files.

What if a support payment is unclear?

Apple processes payments. Check the app’s purchase status information and your Apple purchase history before deciding whether to make another purchase. Do not post receipts or Apple order details in a public issue. Support is optional and never required to create or save.

What should I send with a bug report?

Describe the steps, what you expected, what happened, and the app and iOS versions. Keep it text-only if possible. Do not include personal photos, Apple orders, device identifiers, or unreviewed diagnostic reports.

Get in touch

For questions or a reproducible problem, open a GitHub Issue. A GitHub account is required to submit an issue.

GitHub Issues are public. Do not upload personal photos, Apple order details, device identifiers, or other private material. You do not need to share private media for support. Describe the issue in words; keep sensitive details on your device.
